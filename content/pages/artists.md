      Music is yours. Nāda makes sure it stays that way.

      ## The Problem

      Labels own your masters. Platforms hide payout math. 82% of artists earn less than $30K a year — if even that. You can't see where the money goes or have a say in how the industry runs.

      Nāda is the nonprofit alternative.

      ## 1. Upload and Own

      Drag your MP3 or WAV. Set splits with your collaborators: you 70%, producer 20%, engineer 10% — whatever you agree on. Register your track on the XRP Ledger in seconds. Free. You keep 100% of your rights forever. Nāda owns nothing.

      ## 2. Fans Support You Directly

      Your artist page has a built-in player, a tip button, and subscription options. When a fan tips $5, you see exactly: $4.50 to you, $0.30 to your producer, $0.20 to your engineer. No black box. No surprise deductions.

      ## 3. Get Paid Fast

      Your dashboard shows every incoming payment in real time. Convert your balance to USD anytime. Target payout: 48 hours from support to your bank. One KYC verification, then it's automatic.

      ## 4. Shape the Platform

      Hold NĀDA tokens and vote monthly on:
      - Grant allocation: who gets funding and how much
      - Platform fees and features
      - Ethics and governance decisions

      The DAO is locked inside a nonprofit structure. It cannot be sold, acquired, or flipped.

      ## Your Economics

      | Fan Action | You Receive | Collaborators | Ecosystem Pool |
      |---|---|---|---|
      | $5 tip | $4.50 (90%) | split from your share | 0% |
      | $10/mo subscription | $9.50 (95%) | split from your share | 0% |
      | Project campaign | 90%+ | split from your share | small % |

      Example: 100 fans supporting $5 per month = $450 per month in your bank, after your collaborator splits.
