      Artists and fans connect directly through the NADA Portal, eliminating extractive intermediaries and creating genuine community engagement.

      **NADA** - The source vibration symbolizing origin, intention, and creative sovereignty. Fixed supply of 108,000,000 tokens governing the ecosystem with transparency and trust.

      **NDA Foundation** - 501(c)(3) non-profit stewarding the mission and maintaining legal compliance.
      
      **NDA Trust** - Legal and spiritual custodian of intellectual property and digital assets.
      
      **NDA DAO** - On-chain governance enabling collective decision-making through smart contracts on the XRP Ledger.
      
      ## Core Principles
      
      **Transparency** • Every transaction, vote, and flow of value recorded immutably on-chain
      
      **Sustainability** • Non-extractive tokenomics built for long-term cultural regeneration

      **Cultural Respect** • Protection of sacred, indigenous, and communal art
      
      **Regeneration** • Funds flow back into creation, education, and community
